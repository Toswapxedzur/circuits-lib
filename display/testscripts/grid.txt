# GRID ALIGNMENT: a part's snap terminals must be its REAL studs, so every stud lands on a board socket (pitch 12).
# Regression for "they are not grid aligned" (2026-09-10): T-parts and the 2-row IC were half a pitch off because
# their terminals were derived from the collision-box centre row instead of declared at the studs.
do cursor caught
do clear
do place transistor_npn 36 78 0     # asked half a pitch off in z: snap must move it onto the grid
expect placed == 1
expect part.x.0 == 36
expect part.z.0 == 84               # bar studs (24,84)(48,84) + stem stud (36,72): all sockets
do place ic 48 48 0                 # 2-row IC: its studs are at z = ±6, so the CENTRE sits at a half pitch
expect placed == 2
expect part.x.1 == 48
expect part.z.1 == 54               # studs at z 48 and 60: sockets
do place wire_2 54 24 0             # unchanged standard part: studs ±6 (clear of the IC's footprint)
expect placed == 3
expect part.x.2 == 54
expect part.z.2 == 24
end
